## POME-SHEAF-KUNNETH-FINITE-GENERATION-IFF-001

Severity: low. Status: verified conditional deduction (not a false printed theorem or a proof gap). Targets 10.69.6–9; particularly 10.69.9 lines 169–175. The existing example demonstrates failure for Q. In fact finite generation is exactly the universal criterion, even for arbitrary torsion modules, and the missing part has an explicit cokernel.

**Algebraic lemma.** Let R be a PID, N any R-module, I any set. The canonical map
```
alpha_I: (product_I R) tensor_R N -> product_I N
```
is injective. Its image is precisely the families whose entries lie in a common finitely generated R-submodule of N.

Proof. A tensor is a finite sum of elementary tensors, so its image has the asserted containment. Conversely, if a family lies in the submodule generated by n_1,...,n_s, choose for each coordinate coefficients a_{ij} with n_i' = sum_j a_{ij} n_j; the sum of the s tensors (a_{ij})_i tensor n_j maps to that family. For injectivity write N as the filtered union of its finitely generated submodules M. Every such M is finitely presented over the PID. For a finite presentation R^a -> R^b -> M -> 0, tensor with product_I R and compare with the product of the original presentation. Finite powers commute with products, and products of module exact sequences are exact, by coordinatewise lifts. Thus alpha_I for M is an isomorphism. Every tensor in the domain for N comes from some M, and the comparison to product_I N factors through the injective coordinate inclusion product_I M -> product_I N. A zero image is therefore already a zero tensor for M. No finite generation or flatness of N was assumed. The resulting cokernel is exactly N^I modulo the union of M^I over finitely generated submodules M; this union is directed and hence a submodule.

**Sharpness for a countable test.** If N is not finitely generated, choose n_1,n_2,... recursively with n_{j+1} outside the span of the preceding elements. If their entries lay in a single finitely generated submodule M, the strictly increasing chain of the finite spans inside M would contradict the Noetherian property of M over the PID. Consequently alpha_NaturalNumbers is not onto. Conversely finite generation implies finite presentation and hence an isomorphism for every set I. This also covers N=0, finite torsion and infinitely generated torsion without a denominator argument.

**Sheaf Künneth consequence.** Fix a compact Hausdorff Y and any sheaf B of R-modules on Y. The ordinary external-product short exact sequence of 10.69.8 holds for every topological X and every stalkwise flat sheaf A on X if and only if H^q(Y,B) is finitely generated for every q>=0. Sufficiency is the printed theorem, retained as a conditional internal dependency.

For necessity take X a countably infinite discrete space and A the constant sheaf R. Global sections on X are the product of stalks, an exact functor, so H^p(X,A)=0 for p>0 and H^0(X,A)=R^N. This latter module is torsion-free and hence flat over R. The product space X times Y is the disjoint union of copies of Y, and A box B restricts to B on each copy. A product of the section complexes of the intrinsic Godement resolutions computes its cohomology: restrictions to the open-and-closed components agree with their intrinsic resolutions, and products of module complexes commute with cohomology by exactness of products. Hence H^k(X times Y,A box B)=product_N H^k(Y,B). The proposed sequence has zero Tor term in every degree and no summands with p>0. The actual external map is alpha_NaturalNumbers for H^k(Y,B), by restriction to each component and naturality of the product. It is injective, but onto exactly when that module is finitely generated by the algebraic lemma. Thus the same discrete X detects every degree of failure, and the exact missing quotient is the product modulo the families contained in one finitely generated submodule. This does not require B to be flat, and does not say that finite generation is necessary for each individual X (the compact-X alternative remains valid). The compact-support statement is not refuted: compact supports on a discrete space have finite support, and direct sums commute with tensor.
