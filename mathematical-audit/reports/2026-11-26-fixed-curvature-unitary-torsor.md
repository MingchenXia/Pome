Theorem 10.40.6 constructs a bundle and metric of prescribed integral lift and prescribed curvature but does not classify the choices. The following supplementary classification uses its existence assertion and the local Dolbeault/Poincare lemmas used in 10.40. It is independent of approximation.

Let X be a complex manifold, theta a smooth closed real (1,1)-form with integral real de Rham class, and a an integral lift. Write T_c for the locally constant circle group sheaf, with exact sequence of locally constant sheaves
0 -> Z -> R -> T_c -> 1,
where the exponential is exp(2 pi i x). Distinguish T_c from the sheaf of all smooth circle-valued functions in the printed theorem. Consider holomorphic line bundles with smooth Hermitian metric up to holomorphic isometry.

**Claim.** The set of isometry classes of pairs with curvature theta (and arbitrary integral lift) is a torsor under H^1(X,T_c). The subset with fixed integral class a is a torsor under
H^1(X,R)/im(H^1(X,Z) -> H^1(X,R)).
No compactness is needed here. These are group quotients, with no topology or integral-lattice assertion on arbitrary noncompact X.

Proof: tensor product adds curvatures and integral classes by the metric local formula and the exponential connecting map. The dual negates each. Consequently the difference of two solutions with the same curvature is a Hermitian holomorphic line bundle with zero curvature. Its Chern connection is unitary and flat. Locally take a smooth unit frame, with imaginary connection one-form A. Since dA=0, shrink to a coordinate ball and use the real Poincare lemma to write A=i dg for real g. Multiplying the frame by exp(-ig) makes it parallel; the frame is holomorphic because the (0,1) component of the Chern connection is the holomorphic operator. Two such frames differ by a locally constant unit scalar. Conversely a cocycle of locally constant unit scalars glues trivial Hermitian holomorphic lines with the constant metric, whose Chern connection is flat. Isometries of pairs give locally constant unit changes of these frames, and conversely such changes are isometries; equivalently a holomorphic function of unit modulus on each connected component is constant. The degree-one Cech comparison 10.40.1 then gives the stated H^1 group, including arbitrary covers via refinements. Twisting any one solution by this group is free and transitive by tensor cancellation. Existence of a solution with prescribed lift a is exactly 10.40.6.

The locally constant exponential sequence is exact on stalks. Its inclusion in the smooth circle exponential sequence is identity on Z. Naturality of the long exact sequence says that the integral class of the flat pair is the connecting image of its H^1(T_c) class, with precisely the frame convention of 10.40.2. Exactness at H^1(T_c) identifies the subgroup preserving a with the image of H^1(R), whose kernel is the image of H^1(Z). Also every torsion or other integral class having zero real image is realized by a flat pair, by exactness; no finite-generation assertion is needed. This establishes the fixed-lift torsor.

**Compact connected specialization and fixed-holomorphic-bundle obstruction.** On compact connected X, two metrics on the same holomorphic line bundle of equal curvature have ratio exp(-v) with dd^c v=0. The smooth real pluriharmonic function v is constant by the maximum principle. Multiplication by a constant scalar then gives an isometry between the pairs. In particular forgetting metrics is injective on the fixed-curvature isometry classes. For a fixed holomorphic L and metric h0, a prescribed theta is realized on L itself if and only if theta-c1(L,h0)=dd^c v for a global smooth real v, since h=h0 exp(-v). Equality merely in real de Rham cohomology does not suffice without the printed compact Kahler assumption in 10.40.9.

Explicit countermodel to dropping that assumption, NOT to the actual printed corollary: take the primary scalar Hopf manifold X=(C^n minus {0})/(z~qz), n>=2, q real >1. The holomorphic line bundle obtained by (z,w)~(qz,qw) is smoothly trivial: the positive function |z| defines a nowhere-zero smooth equivariant section. The metric |w|^2/|z|^2 on the cover is invariant, so its curvature is dd^c log |z|^2. This form is semipositive of rank n-1, and is globally d-exact because d^c log |z|^2 is invariant under scaling. If a flat metric on this same L existed, the ratio formula would make dd^c log |z|^2 = dd^c v on X for a globally smooth real v. Such a v is plurisubharmonic and therefore constant on the compact connected X, contradicting nonzero curvature. Thus even for an integrally trivial holomorphic line bundle the fixed-holomorphic-bundle curvature realization can fail on a non-Kahler manifold. The zero target form is nonetheless realized by the trivial holomorphic line, in agreement with 10.40.6. This example also distinguishes holomorphic bundles of zero topological class from unitary-flat holomorphic bundles.
