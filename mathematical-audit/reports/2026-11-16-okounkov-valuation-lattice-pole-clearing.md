## Exact algebraic lemma (with a rational fixed divisor)

Let Y be an integral smooth projective n-fold, A a line bundle, D an effective **rational Cartier divisor** and \(\nu:\mathbb C(Y)^\times\to\mathbb Z^n\) a surjective valuation. Assume the \(\mathbb Q\)-divisor \(A-D\) is **big**, not necessarily nef. Fix any nonzero rational section \(\sigma\) of A, and set
\[
 V_k=H^0(Y,A^k\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-\lceil kD\rceil)),\qquad
 \Gamma_{A,D,\nu,\sigma}
 =\{(\nu(s/\sigma^k),k):k\ge1,\ 0\ne s\in V_k\}.
\]
Then \(\Gamma_{A,D,\nu,\sigma}\) is a semigroup, and the subgroup of \(\mathbb Z^{n+1}\) generated by it is **all of \(\mathbb Z^{n+1}\)**. The conclusion is independent of \(\sigma\): replacing \(\sigma\) by \(g\sigma\) changes \((v,k)\) to \((v-k\nu(g),k)\), an integral shear preserving the full-lattice property.

### Elementary big-divisor auxiliary fact

For every big Cartier divisor B on a projective variety Y and every fixed Cartier divisor T, \(H^0(Y,mB+T)\ne0\) for all sufficiently large **integers** m (not merely on a divisible subsequence).

For completeness: bigness and the usual projective Hilbert-growth estimate give \(rB\sim H+F\) with r positive, H very ample and F effective. One can derive this standard decomposition directly by fixing a very ample H: along a sequence with \(h^0(mB)\ge c m^n\), the restrictions to H have \(h^0(H,mB|_H)=O(m^{n-1})\), so \(H^0(Y,mB-H)\ne0\) for some m; choose r=m. For arbitrary fixed T, write \(m=ar+b\), \(0\le b<r\). Choose a uniform \(a_0\) so that every \(a_0H+bB+T\), for these finitely many b, has a nonzero section (ample twisting). When \(a\ge a_0\), the decomposition
\[
mB+T=(a-a_0)(H+F)+(a_0H+bB+T)+a_0F
\]
produces a nonzero section. Here the Hilbert growth and eventual effectivity of ample twists are basic projective section facts; no analytic regularization theorem is used.

Choose q with \(qD\) integral. Since \(B=q(A-D)\) is big and
\[
kA-\lceil kD\rceil-E=mB+(rA-\lceil rD\rceil-E),\quad k=mq+r,\ 0\le r<q,
\]
the auxiliary fact applies uniformly to the finitely many residue classes. Consequently, for **any** fixed effective Cartier divisor E, \(H^0(Y,kA-\lceil kD\rceil-E)\ne0\) for all sufficiently large k.

### Pole clearing and explicit lattice generators

By surjectivity of \(\nu\), choose rational functions \(f_i\) with \(\nu(f_i)=e_i\) for i=1,...,n. Choose one effective Cartier E large enough that
\[
E+\operatorname{div}(f_i)\ge0\quad\text{for all }i,\qquad
E+\operatorname{div}(\sigma)\ge\lceil D\rceil.
\]
(The divisor of a rational A-section is understood as a Cartier divisor.) Choose a nonzero \(s\in H^0(Y,kA-\lceil kD\rceil-E)\) for some sufficiently large k, and put \(v=\nu(s/\sigma^k)\).

First, \(s,f_1s,\ldots,f_ns\) lie in \(V_k\), because the extra E zeros clear all poles. Their valuation-degree pairs include
\[
(v,k),\qquad(v+e_i,k)\quad(1\le i\le n).
\]
Second, the divisor inequality \(\lceil(k+1)D\rceil\le\lceil kD\rceil+\lceil D\rceil\) implies \(\sigma s\in V_{k+1}\); its normalized valuation is still v, since
\[
\nu((\sigma s)/\sigma^{k+1})=\nu(s/\sigma^k)=v.
\]
Thus the subgroup generated by \(\Gamma\) contains the differences
\[
(e_i,0)=(v+e_i,k)-(v,k),\qquad (0,1)=(v,k+1)-(v,k).
\]
These are precisely the standard generators of \(\mathbb Z^{n+1}\). This proves the claim. The semigroup property follows from the superadditivity of required vanishing orders:
\(\lceil kD\rceil+\lceil lD\rceil\ge\lceil(k+l)D\rceil\). \(\square\)
