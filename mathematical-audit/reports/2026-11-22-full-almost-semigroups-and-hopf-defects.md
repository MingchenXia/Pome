5. **Almost-semigroup extension.** Definition 19.7.31 (`B0C65454CC85`, lines 528–532) is positive-volume only. In Theorem 19.7.32 (`1B239C5F9599`, lines 535–656), choose successive approximants with d_sg-distance at most 2^(-i). For finite intersections S^{i,j}, the contraction inequality gives d_sg(S^{i,j}, S^{i,j+1}) <= 2^(-i-j). The telescoping loss in volume is at most 2^(1-i), so for sufficiently large i every finite intersection has positive volume and Lemma 19.7.25 applies. The saturated decreasing-cone limit T^i has body exactly the intersection of the later bodies. Its increasing union is convex and the limiting volume equals vol S. For any Hausdorff subsequential limit K, this constructed convex body is contained in K, while both have the same *positive* volume; hence equality. The second-choice comparison through S^{i+1} intersect S'^{i+1} and the fast-subsequence continuity argument use only sequential compactness of the **compact metric space of bounded convex bodies**, not unjustified sequential compactness of an arbitrary current space.
