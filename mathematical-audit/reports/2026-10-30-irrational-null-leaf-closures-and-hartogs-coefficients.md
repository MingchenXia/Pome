## POME-NULL-LEAF-KRONECKER-CLOSURE-001

**Status:** low / **verified conditional deduction** (the printed null-leaf theorem and example remain correct; not a repair or a counterexample).

**Pinned integrated anchors:** `docs/sections/integral-leaves-of-constant-rank-null-distributions/index.html`, blob `564667ab5d008b0ffa1d29f5b7ecc578c19d2f04`; §7.15 section tag `930CF3D498A8` (source `klimek-pluripotential-theory:AB3F2174`, physical line 5); Theorem 7.15.3 permanent tag `8FE75D79B9CA`, source `klimek-pluripotential-theory:FB2152E8`, physical lines **51–79**; intrinsic-leaf convention lines **7** and **124–125**. The nearby Example 7.15.5, tag `849194DD5398`, lines 115–120, concerns a *different* semipositivity issue.

**Exact strengthened model statement.** On (Omega=(\mathbb C^*)^2), for a real number (a), let
[
h(z,w)=\log|w|-a\log|z|,\qquad U=h^2.
]
Then (U) is smooth psh with constant complex Hessian rank one, and the global null leaves through ((z_0,w_0)) are
[
L_{z_0,w_0}=\big\{(z_0e^t,w_0e^{at}):t\in\mathbb C\big\}.
]
If (a\notin\mathbb Q), the parametrization is injective and the **exact closure in (Omega)** is the real Levi-flat hypersurface
[
\overline{L_{z_0,w_0}}^{\Omega}=\{(z,w)\in(\mathbb C^*)^2:h(z,w)=h(z_0,w_0)\}.
]
The immersed leaf is not embedded. If (a=p/q\in\mathbb Q) in lowest terms with (q>0), its image is instead the **closed embedded** complex curve
[
L_{z_0,w_0}=\{(z,w):w^qz^{-p}=w_0^qz_0^{-p}\}\simeq\mathbb C^*.
]
Thus rationality is exactly the closed-embedded-leaf criterion for this entire model family; no general compact-leaf criterion is claimed.

**Complete calculation and topological argument.** Since (h) is pluriharmonic,
[
\partial\bar\partial (h^2)=2\partial h\wedge\bar\partial h,
\qquad 2\partial h=dw/w-a\,dz/z,
]
so its complex null line is generated by (z\partial_z+aw\partial_w). Its entire holomorphic flow gives the displayed parametrization. If two parameters have the same image, their difference is (2\pi i k) with (ak\in\mathbb Z). For irrational (a) this forces (k=0). Every point of the leaf has the same value of (h), so its closure is contained in the displayed level set. Conversely, take any ((z,w)) in that level. Fix (x=\log(|z|/|z_0|)), and choose (y_0) matching the argument of (z/z_0). Along (t_k=x+i(y_0+2\pi k)), the first coordinate equals (z), while the second has the desired modulus and its arguments rotate by (2\pi ak). The elementary density of irrational rotations (by the pigeonhole approximation argument) provides a sequence matching the desired second argument. This proves the reverse containment. In particular there are integers (k_j\to\infty) with (e^{2\pi i a k_j}\to1), so intrinsic points (t_j=2\pi i k_j) escape every compact subset of the parameter plane while their ambient images converge to ((z_0,w_0)). The inverse of the inclusion cannot be continuous, proving nonembeddedness. For (a=p/q), the period group is (2\pi i q\mathbb Z), and the monomial equation describes its complete image: when (z) is fixed, changing the choice of (\log(z/z_0)) by (2\pi i k) realizes all (q) roots since (gcd(p,q)=1). Its nonvanishing differential makes it a closed embedded curve, biholomorphic to (\mathbb C/(2\pi i q\mathbb Z)\simeq\mathbb C^*).

**Disposition and dependents.** The printed paragraph already correctly warns about intrinsic topology and nonembedded irrational leaves; this audit newly supplies the *entire closure* and the sharp rational/irrational counterpart. An optional two-sentence example extension beside §7.15 line 124 would make the topology distinction precise. Preserve both the existing theorem and all source/permanent tags. ## POME-HARTOGS-EQUALITY-WITHOUT-EXISTENCE-001

**Status:** low / **verified conditional deduction**. The printed Proposition 9.15.1 is correct; its domain-of-existence hypothesis is *sufficient* for the coefficient identity but not *necessary for a particular function*. This does not allow the hypothesis to be deleted from the universal statement.

**Pinned integrated anchors:** `docs/sections/complete-hartogs-domains-and-taylor-coefficients/index.html`, blob `641a66c419e4a1f7207cd301b51f27f3a6efd6f9`; section tag `F6F3F90BB780`, source `klimek-pluripotential-theory:1253F321`, line **5**; Proposition 9.15.1 permanent `202C805DC8A2`, source `klimek-pluripotential-theory:7CA9E7D7`, statement **8–16**, proof **21–27**.

**Fully explicit model.** Take (D=\mathbb C),
[
u(z)=\max\{0,\log|z|\},\qquad
R(z)=\min\{1,|z|^{-1}\}
]
(with (R(0)=1)). The complete Hartogs domain is
[
H=\{(z,w):|w|<1,\ |zw|<1\},
]
and it is pseudoconvex because (u) is subharmonic. Consider
[
f(z,w)=\frac1{1-w}+\frac1{1-zw}
       =2+\sum_{j\ge1}(1+z^j)w^j\in\mathcal O(H).
]
For (|z|<1), (1+z^j\to1); for (|z|>1), (z^{-j}(1+z^j)\to1), hence
[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\frac1j\log|1+z^j|=
\begin{cases}0,&|z|<1,\\ \log|z|,&|z|>1.\end{cases}
]
At (|z|=1) the upper bound is (j^{-1}\log2\to0). There are infinitely many (j) with (|z^j-1|<1) (periodicity for roots of unity; otherwise the elementary irrational-rotation approximation), and then (|1+z^j|>1). Thus the limsup is also zero on the circle. In fact the **raw pointwise** coefficient limsup already equals (u); taking its upper-semicontinuous regularization changes nothing:
[
\left(\limsup_{j\to\infty}j^{-1}\log|c_j(z)|\right)^*=u(z).
]
Nevertheless (f) is *not* a domain-of-existence function for (H): the boundary point ((0,i)) admits a neighborhood on which both rational summands are holomorphic (their denominators are nonzero). Conversely, the constant function (f=1) on the same (H) has all (c_j=0) for (j\ge1), so its coefficient limsup is (-\infty\ne u). Thus the original sufficient assumption cannot simply be erased for **all** holomorphic functions; it is not a necessary condition for equality for **each** function.

**Disposition and dependents.** Optional note after Proposition 9.15.1 distinguishing an exact radius/coefficient identity from the stronger maximal-analytic-continuation requirement. No correction to the theorem, its proof, or the source labels. This does **not** repeat `POME-HARTOGS-USC-STAR-001`, which used a domain-of-existence example to show the usc star is indispensable. Only the selected coefficient and continuation steps of the integrated proof were checked; the underlying Oka and Hartogs lemmas were not independently certified.
