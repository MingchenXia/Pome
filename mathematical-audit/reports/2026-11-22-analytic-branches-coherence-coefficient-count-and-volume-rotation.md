## POME-LOCAL-COHERENCE-AUXILIARY-COEFFICIENT-COUNT-001: an explicit auxiliary-coefficient bound (low / verified conditional deduction)

**Statement and anchors.** Theorem 9.3.10 (Cartan coherence) has source label `foundations-of-pluripotential-theory:6D85A4A5`, permanent tag `3675C20D7E18`, integrated lines 248–301. Its proof constructs auxiliary functions as coefficients in a parameter `c` of `W_c(z',u_c)` and of `δ_c z_k−B_{kc}(z',u_c)` (lines 257–265), and a nonzero coefficient `h` of `δ_c^m` such that `I_{A,x}=(G_1,\ldots,G_N)_x:h_x` at every point nearby (equation 9.3.11, lines 265–300).

Let a local irreducible branch have base dimension `d`, codimension `r=n−d≥1`, and generic degree `q≥1` under the finite projection of Theorem 9.3.4 (source `foundations-of-pluripotential-theory:62E2CCC8`, permanent `833B11F874F9`, lines 93–183). Write `D=q(q−1)`. The auxiliary ideal in this *specific construction* can be generated by **at most**

\[
N(r,q)=\binom{r+q-1}{q}
+r\binom{r+D-1}{D}
\]

coefficient functions; in particular there is some nonzero holomorphic `h` for which the saturation description of the vanishing ideal uses at most `N(r,q)` auxiliary generators. For `r=0` the prime germ is the ambient germ and its ideal is zero. **This does not assert that the vanishing ideal itself admits `N(r,q)` ordinary generators**, nor does it bound the number of generators of the coherent kernel after taking the colon ideal.

**Complete derivation at the level of the printed construction.** There are `r` components of `c`. For each point of a generic `q`-point fiber, `u_c(z'')` depends linearly on `c`. Therefore the product `W_c(z',u_c)=∏_{i=1}^{q}(u_c(z'')−u_c(z^{(i)}{}''))` is homogeneous of degree `q` in `c`, so has at most \(\binom{r+q-1}{q}\) coefficients. The Vandermonde determinant `D_c` has degree \(q(q-1)/2\) in `c`; its column-`j` replacement `D_{kcj}` has degree \(q(q-1)/2-j\) for \(0\le j<q\). Thus the `j`th term `D_c D_{kcj}u_c^j` of `B_{kc}(z',u_c)` has total degree `D`, exactly as `δ_c=D_c²` does. Hence each `δ_c z_k−B_{kc}(z',u_c)` is homogeneous of degree `D`, with at most \(\binom{r+D-1}{D}\) coefficients, for each of `r` values of `k`. Summing proves the bound (possibly with vanishing or repeated coefficients). For `q=1`, `D=0` and the displayed binomial coefficient has its usual value one. The existing proof of 9.3.10 already shows that one nonzero coefficient `h` of `δ_c^m` yields the colon-ideal identity; no new Oka theorem is invoked here.
