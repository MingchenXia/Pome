Under exactly Theorem 11.16.3's hypotheses (connected smooth projective base of dimension n≥1, algebraic vector bundle of rank r≥2, no smooth strongly pseudoconvex squared Finsler metric with semipositive Kobayashi curvature), put d=n+r−1. There is ONE connected, smooth, locally closed Stein hypersurface S in the punctured total space, such that for every candidate squared Finsler metric G and every integer j=0,...,r, G|S has infinitely many nondegenerate critical points of **real Morse index d−j** and at least r positive Levi eigenvalues. For each j the points can be chosen escaping every compact subset of the total space, with critical values tending to +infinity. No assertion that these are the only critical points or that S is globally closed in the punctured bundle.

Proof. Follow the printed graph construction over B=U × C* × C^(r−2), where U is the dense affine trivializing open. For each G its horizontal Schur complement has a positive direction by the curvature sign in §11.12 lines 19–25. Its vertical Hessian is positive; choose a point v0 over U with t nonzero and G_w nonzero, as in 11.16.3 line 65 (G_w cannot vanish on any open set because G_{w bar w}>0). The Euler identities in Lemma 11.12.1 (source 14B27C66/permanent 82E28EBCB904; lines 42–48 of docs/sections/gauduchon-metrics-and-linear-mass--convex-norms-and-gauduchon-degrees--polar-norms-of-semipositive-finsler-metrics/index.html, blob 9d8c639c4460719360048213e58aa08138dd1a71) show that the tangent hyperplane ker ∂G is the Levi-orthogonal sum of the horizontal space and the codimension-one vertical tangent. Its Levi form has at least r positive eigenvalues.

Fix the value and first jet of the graph w=g(b) so that its tangent is that hyperplane. F=G∘Graph(g) is critical there. The Hermitian mixed second derivative matrix is fixed by this first jet. In complex linear coordinates diagonalizing that matrix, write the quadratic Taylor part as sum λ_i|z_i|² + Re sum a_i z_i². Every choice of complex a_i is realizable by a holomorphic quadratic jet of g: F_{αβ}=A_{αβ}+G_w g_{αβ}, and G_w≠0 (line 78). Choose j of the positive λ_i and set their a_i=0. On each other coordinate choose a_i real and >|λ_i|. The real Hessian on each selected coordinate has two positive eigenvalues 2λ_i; on every other coordinate it has eigenvalues 2(λ_i+a_i), 2(λ_i−a_i) of opposite sign. Thus the Hessian is invertible and has exactly d−j negative eigenvalues. The Levi form has not changed. This works also if some unselected λ_i vanish, and d≥r because n≥1.

Choose compact C'⊂int C around the critical point in B, with C holomorphically convex and t(C) in the small disk from 11.16.2. For this index j, the same C² stability of a nondegenerate critical point and openness of fixed nondegenerate real inertia and of at least r positive Levi eigenvalues provide the metric neighborhood and the graph tolerance of line 83. In more detail, choose a small real coordinate ball with the derivative of the gradient near the invertible Hessian H at the center. For every sufficiently C²-close function the map x↦x−H^{-1}∇F_new(x) is a strict contraction on a smaller closed ball and maps that ball into itself. Its fixed point is critical, and the new Hessian there stays close to H. Thus no smooth dependence on a parameter is needed. Holomorphic Cauchy estimates on C' supply the C² control of the graph.

For each of the finitely many j, the candidate metric space on the compact sphere bundle is second countable in its C² topology, so take a countable subcover of its test neighborhoods. Form one countable list of all these tests, repeating every test infinitely often. Apply the induction in Lemma 11.16.2 to this list with the additional requirement R_l>l at stage l (besides R_l>4M/3 at line 37). Neither the separated-disks argument nor the summable approximation estimates change. Its dilated sets have |t|≥3R_l/4, hence their t-coordinates tend to infinity. Dilation preserves the real Morse index and Levi inertia because G∘D_λ=|λ|²G and the graph reparametrization is biholomorphic. The resulting single graph supplies, for each G and j, critical points in the infinitely repeated tests of some covering neighborhood. Their t-coordinates escape to infinity on U. To ensure escape in the *total bundle* (the trivialization may be unbounded near X\U), note that each repeated test uses a fixed compact original C'_a over a compact subset of U. On that compact base subset the background Hermitian norm is bounded below by a positive multiple of |t|. Also min G on the compact global unit sphere bundle is positive, so G at these points tends to infinity. This proves the asserted escape and critical values. The graph is smooth, Stein, connected and locally closed by the original argument, not necessarily closed globally.
