## POME-SYMMETRIC-POWER-GENERATION-GCD-001

Severity low; status verified conditional deduction (not a printed error). Applies to Definition 10.93.4, line 54, using the jet construction in Proposition 10.93.3, lines 30–49. No compactness, curvature hypothesis, or external positivity theorem is needed. The proposed optional complement is an exact arithmetic criterion for the two eventual quantifiers; no downstream theorem is refuted.

Let E have positive rank on a complex manifold X. Write G for the set of positive integers k for which Sym^k E is globally generated, and V for those k for which Sym^k E is very ample in the exact first-jet and distinct-pair sense of the definition. Then:

1. G+G is contained in G. Also V+G is contained in V, and V is contained in G.
2. E is semiample in the printed sense iff G is nonempty and gcd(G)=1. Equivalently, some finite list of globally generated positive powers has gcd one.
3. E is ample in the printed sense iff V is nonempty and G is nonempty with gcd(G)=1. Equivalently, a single first-jet-and-pair very ample power and finitely many globally generated powers of gcd one suffice.

Proof: the symmetric multiplication Sym^a E tensor Sym^b E -> Sym^(a+b) E is fiberwise onto, since every monomial of degree a+b factors into monomials of degrees a and b. Products of global sections therefore span the target at a point if both powers are globally generated. If A=Sym^a E is very ample and B=Sym^b E globally generated, at a point x choose global sections b_1,...,b_d of B forming a frame on a neighborhood of x (their determinant is nonzero at x). The local bundle map A^d -> Sym^(a+b)E, (u_i) -> sum u_i b_i, is surjective there, and admits a local holomorphic right inverse by choosing a nonzero maximal minor. Consequently it is surjective on first jets. Given a prescribed target first jet, lift it to first jets of A^d and realize each by a global section of A. Multiplication by the selected b_i realizes the target. At distinct x,y, choose global sections of B spanning B_x and possibly different ones spanning B_y. At x express the desired target value by products; choose sections of A with the specified value at x and zero at y, using pair surjectivity. Do the reverse at y. The sum realizes both values. Hence V+G ⊂ V. Jet surjectivity implies ordinary point generation by forgetting derivatives.

If G is nonempty and gcd(G)=1, a finite subset a_1,...,a_l already has gcd one (the gcd of successive finite subsets is a decreasing sequence of positive divisors, eventually constant). In the finite cyclic group Z/a_1 Z the additive monoid generated by the other residues equals the subgroup they generate: negatives are nonnegative multiples by finite order. The subgroup is the whole group. For each residue j choose a nonnegative combination b_j of the a_i representing it (allow 0 for residue zero). Every integer k≥max_j b_j+a_1 can be written b_j+t a_1 with t≥0 and at least one positive summand. Hence every sufficiently large k belongs to G. Necessity is immediate from two consecutive sufficiently large degrees. If v belongs to V, then v+G contains every sufficiently large integer, proving the ample criterion. Conversely eventual very ampleness implies the stated conditions. This uses ordinary finite arithmetic, not a compactness or asymptotic assumption.

Sharp warning about one generated power: take the elliptic curve C/(Z+iZ) and the holomorphic flat line bundle L associated with the character sending 1 to −1 and i to 1. L² is trivial. For every odd k, any section of L^k lifts to an entire function f with f(z+1)=−f(z) and f(z+i)=f(z). Its absolute value is periodic and bounded on C, so Liouville makes f constant and hence zero. For every even k the power is trivial and globally generated. Thus G=2Z_{>0}; L is not semiample in the printed eventual convention, despite one generated power. The definition should not be silently replaced by existence of a single generated power. No claim about other conventions for vector-bundle positivity.

## POME-JET-BUNDLE-DETERMINANT-001

Severity low; status verified conditional deduction. Proposition 10.93.3, lines 30–49. Optional complement; no printed theorem changes. Let E have rank r, X have complex dimension n, K_X=det Omega_X^1. For k≥0,
 
det J^k E ≅ (det E)^{binom(n+k,n)} tensor K_X^{r binom(n+k,n+1)}.

This includes k=0 (the second exponent vanishes); for n=0, K_X is the trivial exterior-zero-power line.

Proof: the kernel of forgetting the highest-order terms is canonically Sym^k Omega_X^1 tensor E, by the Taylor-coordinate model in the proposition. A degree-k homogeneous leading term transforms by the linear part of the coordinate change and the value of the frame-change matrix; all higher derivatives and frame terms multiply a zero lower-order term or contribute order >k. Thus for k≥1 there is a short exact sequence of holomorphic bundles
0 -> Sym^k Omega_X^1 tensor E -> J^k E -> J^(k−1) E -> 0.
For n≥1, det Sym^j F=(det F)^{binom(n+j−1,n)} for a rank-n bundle F and j≥1: on diagonal invertible matrices the weight of each eigenvalue in the determinant is j/n times the number binom(n+j−1,n−1) of monomials, equal to binom(n+j−1,n). The identity extends to all invertible complex matrices by density of matrices with distinct eigenvalues, hence applies to transition functions and gives the line-bundle identity. For j=0 the symmetric power is the trivial line. The tensor determinant identity follows likewise from diagonal matrices (or local bases). Multiplicativity of determinant in the exact sequences now gives the exponent of det E as the sum of binom(n+j−1,n−1) for j=0,...,k, namely binom(n+k,n), and the exponent of K_X as r times the sum of binom(n+j−1,n) for j=1,...,k, namely r binom(n+k,n+1). Both sums follow by Pascal's identity. If n=0, all positive-order homogeneous pieces vanish and J^kE=E, giving the same formula directly. Taking c1 gives the corresponding integral first Chern class identity without any splitting-principle hypothesis. No curvature sign or positivity of jet bundles asserted.
