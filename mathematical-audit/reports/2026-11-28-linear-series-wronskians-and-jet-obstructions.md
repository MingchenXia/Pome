## POME-CURVE-LINEAR-SERIES-WRONSKIAN-PULLBACK-001

Let X be a connected compact Riemann surface, E a holomorphic line bundle of degree d, and V a nonzero (r+1)-dimensional subspace of H^0(X,E), not necessarily complete or base-point-free. Set N=r(r+1)/2. In a holomorphic frame e and coordinate z, write a basis as s_j=F_j e, j=0,...,r. Define W_V=det(F_j^(k)) e^(r+1)(dz)^N, with rows k=0,...,r. Globally this is a nonzero section of E^(r+1) tensor K_X^N, well-defined up to nonzero constant from the basis choice.

Indeed under a change e'=h e the functions are divided by h, with h nowhere zero. The Leibniz rule gives a triangular row transformation with diagonal h^(-1). The determinant scales by h^(-(r+1)), exactly canceled by the frame factor. Under z=z(w) the chain rule gives triangular rows with diagonal (z')^k, determinant (z')^N. These two checks prove the gluing independently. For a fixed x the filtration of V by vanishing order has graded pieces of dimension at most one (Taylor coefficient) and terminates because a nonzero section has zero divisor of degree d. Choose a basis with distinct initial orders 0<=a_0<...<a_r<=d, and normalize leading coefficients to one. Factoring z^(a_j) from column j and z^(-k) from row k away from zero yields determinant z^(sum a_j-N) times a holomorphic function with nonzero constant det((a_j)_k), the Vandermonde product, as in the printed proof. Thus W_V is nonzero, with exact vanishing order w_V(x)=sum(a_j-j)>=0. In particular
`sum_x w_V(x)=(r+1)d+r(r+1)(g_X-1)`.
This holds with fixed base divisor included; subtracting a common effective divisor B of the series replaces each a_j by a_j-ord_x B, and subtracts (r+1)B from the Wronskian divisor. No unproved positivity of a line bundle is used.

For a nonconstant holomorphic f:Y->X of connected compact curves and V'=f^*V inside H^0(Y,f^*E), pullback is injective by surjectivity of f. Write the local map z=z(t) and e_x(f) for its local degree at y. The same triangular chain-rule matrix gives locally W_{V'}=f^*(W_V) multiplied by (dz/dt)^N in the chosen frames (intrinsically, apply (df)^N to the cotangent factors). Hence
`div W_{V'}=f^*(div W_V)+N R_f`,
where R_f=sum_y(e_y(f)-1)[y]. Equivalently w_{V'}(y)=e_y(f) w_V(f(y))+N(e_y(f)-1), also directly from the vanishing sequence a'_j=e_y a_j. Degrees agree with the printed Riemann–Hurwitz and degree-pullback formula. For a pencil r=1 this gives the usual ramification divisor after removing base points. For E=K_X and V=H^0(K_X), r=g_X-1, it gives precisely the printed total canonical weight (the frame factor supplies the additional g_X powers of K_X). Pullback of a canonical series here lives in f^*K_X, not the full canonical series on Y; no identification of those series is asserted. No correction to 10.78.17 required. Optional addition next to that theorem; no dependent theorem refuted.

## POME-CURVE-JET-EXACT-DEFECT-001

Low / verified conditional deduction. Target same file, Lemma 10.78.1 source `529667C6`, tag `370B2D433170`, lines 20–47; Theorem 10.78.9 source `B4C3A1F9`, tag `7F8CC797059F`, lines 133–162; Corollary 10.78.11 source `76995AC9`, tag `43B79368A1B8`, lines 167–184.

For an effective nonzero divisor D of degree m, consider the jet evaluation H^0(E)->H^0(E/E(-D)). Apply 10.78.1 to F=E(-D), so the quotient has dimension m, is supported at the points of D and the long exact sequence identifies the cokernel with the kernel of H^1(E(-D))->H^1(E). By the perfect natural analytic Serre pairing of 10.78.9 (naturality follows directly from the integral pairing and the inclusion of coefficient line bundles), its dual is
`H^0(K_X tensor E^* (D)) / H^0(K_X tensor E^*)`.
In particular the defect dimension is their dimension difference, not just an upper bound. If deg E>=2g-1+m both spaces vanish. At the boundary deg E=2g-2+m the second space has negative degree and the first degree zero. The degree-zero criterion in 10.78.7 shows the evaluation is surjective except exactly when E is isomorphic to K_X(D); then its cokernel is one-dimensional. This is sharp as a uniform degree threshold for all line bundles and nonzero D of given length m on a fixed curve (take E=K_X(D) at the preceding degree). Also h^1(E)=0 at that boundary because m>0.

There is a parallel exact principal-part obstruction for H^0(E(D))->H^0(E(D)/E): the dual cokernel is H^0(K_X tensor E^*)/H^0(K_X tensor E^*(-D)), obtained from the same exact sequence. This records actual obstructions, rather than replacing the original sufficient estimate for the existence of a pole. No external approximation input.
