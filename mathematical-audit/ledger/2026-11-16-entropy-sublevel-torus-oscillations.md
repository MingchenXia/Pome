- **Exact new result:** on the unit-volume elliptic torus with Kähler \(\theta=\mathrm dV\), smooth uniformly elliptic metrics \((1+a\cos2\pi kx)\theta=\theta+\mathrm dd^cu_k\) have \(u_k\to0\) in \(C^1\). Their unique supporting probability densities \(h_k=1+a\cos2\pi kx\) all have the same explicit dual entropy \(1-\sqrt{1-a^2}+\log((1+\sqrt{1-a^2})/2)\), which can be arbitrarily close to the minimum \(0\), yet \(h_k\rightharpoonup1\) weakly in \(L^1\) while \(\|h_k-1\|_1=2a/\pi\) and total-variation distance is \(a/\pi\). Thus for **every** \(L>0\), the concrete \(\mathcal K_L\) is not norm relatively compact; \(\mathcal K_0=\{\mathrm dV\}\).
- **Limits and dependencies:** all estimates for the concrete model are calculated directly with \(\mathrm dd^c=(\mathrm i/2\pi)\partial\bar\partial\), Stokes/Jensen and a Fourier integral. General Kähler/closed-prescribed-type dual identification remains a cited internal premise, not transitively certified. No contradiction with the printed weak-compactness theorem or the bounded-\(L^1\) stability theorem: the oscillating input logarithmic densities do not converge in \(L^1\).
