# Classification: spherical Riesz flux and atomic recovery

**POME-RIESZ-SPHERICAL-FLUX-ATOMIC-RECOVERY-001** — **low / verified conditional deduction** (2026-11-10; reviewed main b6933b772f41b45918f7e25ed1afbbb1fd892272).

For locally integrable subharmonic \(u\) in real dimension \(d\ge2\), with \(\mu=\Delta u\), the one-sided derivatives of its spherical mean satisfy \(s_{d-1}r^{d-1}m'_-(r)=\mu(B(a,r))\) and \(s_{d-1}r^{d-1}m'_+(r)=\mu(\overline{B(a,r)})\). The jump is the exact mass of the sphere. Its normalized small-radius limit recovers \(\mu(\{a\})\); the \(\log r\) coefficient for \(d=2\) and \(r^{2-d}\) coefficient for \(d\ge3\) have the constants of equation 3.1.14. A discrete compactly supported measure with atoms converging to but not at \(a\) shows \(u(a)=-\infty\) with \(\mu(\{a\})=0\).
