5.55 Integration over real submanifolds22A12C309D37

22A12C309D37

Proposition 5.55.1.E09F702EB680 Let $M$ be an oriented smooth real manifold of dimension $m$, and let $Z\subset M$ be a closed oriented embedded submanifold of class $C^1$ and dimension $p$, possibly with boundary. Integration defines an order-zero current $[Z]$ of degree $m-p$, with support $Z$. With the outward-normal-first boundary orientation and the convention $\langle \mathrm dT,\alpha \rangle =(-1)^{\deg T+1} \langle T,\mathrm d\alpha \rangle $, one has

\[ \mathrm d[Z]=(-1)^{m-p+1}[\partial Z]. \]

In particular $[Z]$ is normal, and it is closed if $\partial Z$ is empty.

Proof. In a $C^1$ parameterization $F$ of $Z$, pullback of a continuous $p$-form involves only the coefficients of the form and the first derivatives of $F$. A compact subset of $M$ meets $Z$ in a compact set, covered by finitely many relatively compact parameter patches. The continuous Jacobians are bounded on those patches. A partition of unity therefore gives, for every compact $K\subset M$,

\[ \left|\int _ Z\alpha \right|\leq C_ K\| \alpha \| _{C^0(K)} \qquad (\operatorname {Supp}\alpha \subset K). \]

Here use finitely many fixed ambient coordinates for the coefficient norm. Change of variables for $C^1$ parameterizations makes the integral independent of the charts. This proves the order-zero assertion. Its support is contained in $Z$ by closedness. Near every interior point one can choose a smooth ambient $p$-form whose pullback is positive on a small oriented parameter patch. Thus the current is nonzero there. Boundary points are limits of interior points, giving equality of the support with $Z$.

We supply the required $C^1$ Stokes argument, since the pullback of a smooth form by a merely $C^1$ parameterization need not be $C^1$. Localize a smooth compactly supported $(p-1)$-form $\alpha $ by an ambient smooth partition of unity near $Z\cap \operatorname {Supp}\alpha $. It suffices to treat one parameter patch, with fixed parameter domain an open box or a half-box $\{ s_ p\geq 0\} $. The pullback has compact support away from all artificial sides. Work in one ambient coordinate chart. The coordinate map $F$ has a $C^1$ extension across the flat boundary: for negative $t=s_ p$ put

\[ F(s',t)=3F(s',-t)-2F(s',-2t) \]

on a smaller box. The value and all first derivatives match at $t=0$, including the normal derivative $-3+4=1$. Mollification yields smooth maps $F_ j\to F$ in $C^1$ on the compact parameter region in question, with values in the same ambient chart. They need not be embeddings. For all sufficiently large $j$, the pullbacks of the localized form have support in one fixed compact parameter region away from the artificial sides. Indeed, choose the ambient support compactly inside the original patch and a larger compact parameter neighborhood; on its boundary the image of $F$ is separated from that support, and uniform convergence preserves the separation.

Smooth pullback commutes with $\mathrm d$. The smooth half-box Stokes formula, already proved by coordinate Fubini in Stokes' formula on compact domains with corners, gives

\[ \int F_ j^*(\mathrm d\alpha ) =\int _{s_ p=0}F_ j^*\alpha \]

with the induced orientation; in an interior patch the right side is zero. Uniform $C^1$ convergence gives uniform convergence of both pullbacks on these fixed compact sets. Passing to the limit proves the same equality for $F$. Summing the ambient partition cancels its differentiated coefficients and proves $\int _ Z\mathrm d\alpha =\int _{\partial Z}\alpha $. The displayed current sign now follows from the stated differential convention. Since $\partial Z$ is a closed $C^1$ submanifold of dimension $p-1$, its integration current is of order zero by the same chart estimate. Thus $[Z]$ and its derivative both have order zero. If $p=0$, compact sets meet the closed discrete submanifold in finitely many points; the current is the oriented point sum, and its derivative and boundary both vanish. $\square$
