16.44 Pointwise estimates for obstacle envelopes476967CD375B

476967CD375B

Lemma 16.44.1.8281BE55D0D3 Let $(Z,\kappa )$ be compact Kähler, of dimension $m$. There is a constant $A>0$, depending only on this fixed metric, such that, for smooth real $f$ and $v=P_\kappa (f)$,

\[ 0\leq m+\Delta _\kappa v \leq 2\bigl (m+\sup _ Z\Delta _\kappa f\bigr ) \exp \bigl (A(f-\min _ Zf)\bigr ) \]

in the sense of distributions. Here $\Delta _\kappa =\operatorname {tr}_\kappa dd^ c$ has the complex-geometric sign. If $f=g-\psi $, with $g$ smooth and $\psi $ quasi-psh with a logarithmic pole set, smooth off that set, and $dd^ c\psi \geq -C\kappa $, the same estimate holds off the poles, with $m+\sup \Delta f$ replaced by $(C+1)m+\sup \Delta g$ and $\min f$ replaced by $\inf f$. The same conclusion holds when $\psi $ is a sum of positive real logarithmic divisor potentials and a smooth function; this is the resolved form used below.

Proof. Use the existing smooth penalized equation and its uniform convergence to the envelope. In the notation of its trace proof, with reference and background metric both equal to $\kappa $, put $H_\beta =m+\Delta _\kappa a_\beta $ and $Q_\beta =\log H_\beta -Aa_\beta $. The maximum-principle proof of the trace estimate for the penalized Kähler equation gives, for all sufficiently large $\beta $, $H_\beta (x_\beta )\leq 2B$ at a maximum point of $Q_\beta $, where $B=m+\sup \Delta _\kappa f$. Retain the difference of the two values of $a_\beta $ and use the lower bound $a_\beta (x_\beta )\geq \min f$ to get

\[ H_\beta (x)\leq 2B\exp \bigl (A(a_\beta (x)-\min f)\bigr ). \]

Uniform convergence $a_\beta \to v$, followed by $v\leq f$, gives the asserted variable upper bound after testing against a nonnegative smooth function. Positivity of $\kappa +dd^ cv$ gives its lower bound.

For the logarithmic obstacle, first note that its envelope is bounded. A constant gives a lower bound. For an upper bound, choose a nonempty open set on which $f$ is bounded above. The uniform $L^1$ bound for normalized $\kappa $-psh functions bounds the supremum of every competitor by its upper bound on that open set plus a fixed constant. Thus the envelope has a finite global upper bound as well. If $R>\sup (v-g)$, then $v\leq g-\max (\psi ,-R)$. Replace this maximum by a smooth regularized maximum $\psi _ R$ satisfying

\[ \psi \leq \psi _ R\leq \max (\psi ,-R),\qquad dd^ c\psi _ R\geq -C\kappa . \]

Increasing $R$ by a fixed regularization margin ensures these inequalities. Choose it also so large that on the truncated region $g-\psi _ R\geq \inf (g-\psi )$; it suffices to take $R>\inf (g-\psi )-\min g+2$, with the regularization width fixed to be less than one. The two obstacle inequalities show that $P_\kappa (g-\psi _ R)=v$. The smooth result applies, and $\Delta (g-\psi _ R)\leq \Delta g+Cm$ while $\inf (g-\psi _ R)=\inf (g-\psi )$. On each compact set outside the poles, $\psi _ R=\psi $ for large $R$. This proves the last assertion, with constants independent of $R$. $\square$

We use the metric-Laplacian bound for rooftop envelopes, the mixed contact-density identity, the contact density of an envelope with a regular obstacle, and Bedford–Taylor continuity under convergence in capacity.
