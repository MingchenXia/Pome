10.108 Tautological metrics and maximal slopes62919C7A7278

62919C7A7278

Ambient manifolds are connected unless stated otherwise. Fibers need not be connected; the section-envelope arguments treat their connected components separately.

Let $\mathcal E$ be a reflexive sheaf of rank $r>0$ on compact Kähler $X$ and $X_0$ its locally free locus. Choose a compact smooth model $P$ of $\mathbb P(\mathcal E|_{X_0})$ and a Hermitian form $\Omega _ P$. We call $\mathcal E$ approximately pseudo-effective if for every $\varepsilon >0$ the tautological quotient bundle on this open set has a singular metric of curvature at least $-\varepsilon \Omega _ P$. Changing the smooth metric on this fixed model does not affect the condition, by uniform comparison. Passing to a dominating smooth compact model preserves it by pullback. It does not assert existence of a semipositive limiting metric on this noncompact open set.

Lemma 10.108.1.C0B465266C15 On $\mathbb P^{r-1}$ let $\theta $ be Fubini–Study and let $h$ be a metric on $\mathcal O(1)$ of curvature at least $-\varepsilon C\theta $. If $m\varepsilon C\leq 1$ and $N\geq r+1$, then

\[ H^0(\mathbb P^{r-1},K_{\mathbb P^{r-1}}\otimes \mathcal O(m+N)\otimes \mathcal I(h^ m))\ne 0. \]

More generally the same conclusion holds for a smooth auxiliary metric $h_ a$ on $\mathcal O(N)$ whenever $c_1(\mathcal O(m+N),h^ m h_ a)\geq r\theta $.

Proof. Endow $\mathcal O(m+N)$ with $h^ m h_{\rm FS}^ N$. Its curvature is at least $(N-1)\theta $. Choose a point $x$ outside the zero set of $\mathcal I(h^ m)$. For $r>1$, the $r-1$ hyperplanes cutting out $x$ give a global logarithmic weight $\eta _ x$ with curvature at least $-(r-1)\theta $ and pole $(r-1)\log |z-x|^2+O(1)$ at $x$. In either formulation, adding $\eta _ x$ leaves strictly positive curvature. Its multiplier ideal equals $\mathcal I(h^ m)$ away from $x$ and is contained in the maximal ideal at $x$, by the pole threshold and upper boundedness of the original weight. The original ideal is $\mathcal O$ near $x$. Nadel vanishing for the new ideal and the quotient exact sequence therefore supply a global section of the original ideal with nonzero value at $x$. This proves the assertion. For $r=1$ the projective fiber is a point and the assertion is immediate. $\square$

Theorem 10.108.2.9CD904E0945C If $\mathcal E$ is approximately pseudo-effective, then $\mu _{\alpha ,\max }(\mathcal E)\geq 0$ for every movable class $\alpha $. Equivalently, positive minimum slope of a reflexive sheaf implies that its dual is not approximately pseudo-effective.

Proof. Choose a projective modification $f:\widehat X\to X$, obtained by smooth-center blowups, on which $E=f^*\mathcal E/\text{torsion}$ is locally free. Take a common smooth resolution $Q$ of $\mathbb P(E)$ and the chosen model $P$, projective over $\widehat X$, with $q:Q\to \mathbb P(E)$ and $\pi :Q\to \widehat X$. All maps are isomorphisms on the original projective bundle over $X_0$. We do not require a morphism from $\mathbb P(E)$ itself to an arbitrary chosen resolution. Fix a relatively ample line bundle $A$ on $Q$. A smooth metric on it has curvature $\theta _ A$ with $\theta _ A+C_0\pi ^*\widehat\omega >0$. Increasing its fixed power if necessary, its vertical curvature dominates $(r+1)$ times the Fubini–Study form pulled back from $\mathbb P(E)$. Indeed this comparison holds uniformly on compact $Q$ after a base form is added.

The pullback of the error form $\Omega _ P$ is bounded by a fixed multiple of $\theta _ A+C_0\pi ^*\widehat\omega $. For each $m$, take $\varepsilon _ m$ so small that its product with $m$ and this bound leaves both a positive vertical margin and a base lower bound $-C_1\pi ^*\widehat\omega $, with $C_1$ independent of $m$. Then $h_{\varepsilon _ m}^ m h_ A$ on $q^*\mathcal O(m)\otimes A$ has that lower bound; its ideal is $\mathcal I(h_{\varepsilon _ m}^ m)$. On the open bundle locus write $A=\mathcal O(N)\otimes \pi ^*B$ with fixed $N\geq r+1$. Here $B$ extends to a line bundle on $\widehat X$: first $(q_*A)^{**}$ is a line bundle on the smooth $\mathbb P(E)$, and its projective-bundle Picard decomposition has this form; the difference upstairs is supported on the exceptional divisors.

On $f^{-1}(X_0)$ consider

\[ G_ m=\pi _*\bigl (K_{Q/\widehat X}\otimes q^*\mathcal O(m)\otimes A\otimes \mathcal I(h_{\varepsilon _ m}^ m)\bigr ). \]

The fiber lemma makes this sheaf nonzero. More precisely choose a general base point where the multiplier ideal restricts to the fiber ideal, outside the exceptional set furnished by Fubini and the restriction/extension theorem for multiplier ideals. Fiberwise $L^2$ extension then identifies the nonzero fiber space with locally extendable sections. These are the generic base-change and extension conditions used here.

The singular $L^2$ direct-image theorem, applied after a local base twist with curvature $C_1\widehat\omega $, gives a metric on $G_ m$ with Griffiths curvature at least $-C_1\widehat\omega \otimes \operatorname {Id}$. For this multiplier-ideal direct image the exact external input is [Theorem 21.1, HPS2017], in arXiv:1611.08768v2; determinant positivity follows from lemma 10.104.1. The generic-isomorphism restriction of the earlier Păun–Takayama formulation is not assumed automatically. In particular,

\[ c_1(\det G_ m,\det h_ m)\geq -C_1\operatorname {rk}(G_ m)\widehat\omega . \]

We now descend the direct image and its determinant metric. In the quotient convention, $K_{\mathbb P(E)/\widehat X}=\mathcal O(-r)\otimes \pi ^*\det E$. The fixed $B$ descends, modulo exceptional divisors, to a line bundle $M$ on smooth $X$, by the same reflexive-pushforward argument. Thus $G_ m$ is a subsheaf of $M\otimes \det \mathcal E\otimes \operatorname {Sym}^{m+N-r}\mathcal E$ on $X_0$. Saturate it there. Its Grassmannian map extends meromorphically across $X\setminus X_0$ by lemma 9.72.1, giving a coherent saturated subsheaf $\overline G_ m$ of the reflexive ambient sheaf on $X$. Saturation changes its determinant by an effective divisor, so the determinant metric's lower curvature bound persists after adding that divisor metric.

The error can be transferred to $X$ using a fixed scalar weight. The existing exceptional correction lemma gives an effective rational exceptional divisor $D$ and a Kähler form $\gamma $ in $f^*[\omega _ X]-[D]$. Choose component metrics, with combined curvature $\Theta _ D$ and logarithmic section norm $L_ D$, so that $dd^ cL_ D=[D]-\Theta _ D$. The $dd^ c$-lemma gives a smooth $u$ with $\gamma =f^*\omega _ X-\Theta _ D+dd^ cu$. Hence

\[ f^*\omega _ X+dd^ c(L_ D+u)=\gamma +[D]\geq 0. \]

By the existing plurisubharmonic descent theorem, $L_ D+u=f^*\rho $ for a global $\omega _ X$-psh function $\rho $. It is smooth off the exceptional image, where $f^*(\omega _ X+dd^ c\rho )=\gamma $. Uniform comparison of the two fixed Kähler forms on compact $\widehat X$ yields $C_2$ with

\[ \widehat\omega \leq C_2 f^*(\omega _ X+dd^ c\rho ) \]

off that image. All these choices are fixed before $m$ varies.

Put $c=C_1C_2\operatorname {rk}(G_ m)$ and add $c\rho $ to the determinant weight on $X_0$. Its curvature is then at least $-c\omega _ X$. After a smooth local potential is added, it is plurisubharmonic. Codimension-two removal extends it to a metric on $\det \overline G_ m$. Thus

\[ c_1(\det \overline G_ m) +C_1C_2\operatorname {rk}(G_ m)[\omega _ X] \]

is pseudo-effective. Pair this class directly with the given movable class $\alpha $. By the definition of the dual cone,

\[ \mu _\alpha (\overline G_ m) \geq -C_1C_2\, \alpha \cdot [\omega _ X]=-C_3, \]

where $C_3$ is independent of $m$. This cohomological pairing applies equally to interior and boundary movable classes.

Let $d=m+N-r$. The ambient symmetric power is a subsheaf of the tensor power in characteristic zero. Tensor slope additivity consequently gives

\[ -C_3\leq \mu _\alpha (\overline G_ m) \leq \deg _\alpha M+\deg _\alpha (\det \mathcal E) +d\mu _{\alpha ,\max }(\mathcal E). \]

Let $m\to \infty $. This proves the assertion for every movable class. Duality gives the final formulation of the theorem. $\square$
