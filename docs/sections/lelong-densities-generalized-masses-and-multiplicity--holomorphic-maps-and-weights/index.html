6.35 Holomorphic maps and weights551B3E18C92F

551B3E18C92F

Proposition 6.35.1.9BBFC6858DCC Let $F:X\to Y$ be holomorphic and proper on $S=\operatorname {supp}T$, with $T$ closed positive of bidimension $(p,p)$. Let $\psi $ be an admissible weight on $Y$, semi-exhaustive on $F(S)$, and suppose $\psi \circ F$ is an admissible weight on $X$. Then

\[ M_{F_*T}(\psi ,r)=M_ T(\psi \circ F,r),\qquad \nu (F_*T,\psi )=\nu (T,\psi \circ F). \]

If $p>\dim Y$, both sides are zero. Holomorphic tuple weights $\log \sum |f_ j|^2$ are covered whenever their low support sublevels are relatively compact. Locally comparable norms of two tuples with the same zero set give the same number; this applies, in particular, to two locally bounded holomorphic changes of generators with locally bounded inverse changes.

Proof. For a continuous locally bounded PSH function $u$ on $Y$ and a positive order-zero current $R$ with proper support, the integral definition of pushforward gives $F_*((u\circ F)R)=uF_*R$. One may first use smooth scalar functions and then their local uniform approximations; compact inverse images bound every coefficient error. Commutation of pushforward with $dd^ c$ in pushforward of currents proper on their support and bounded recursion then give, inductively,

\[ F_*\bigl (T\wedge (dd^ c(u\circ F))^ j\bigr ) =F_*T\wedge (dd^ cu)^ j. \]

Take $u=\max \{ \psi ,s\} $. Properness and semi-exhaustion give compact low support sublevels upstairs. Pushforward of the resulting top-degree measure and integration over $B_\psi (r)$ prove the mass identity; then take the limit. If $p>\dim Y$, the pushforward current is zero by degree and the positive pushed-forward top measure is zero. It follows that the source top measure is zero as well: every compact source set has compact image, near which one can choose a target test function equal to one. Tuple weights are PSH by plurisubharmonicity of logarithmic holomorphic norms. Comparable tuple norms give bounded differences of their logarithms, so corollary 6.34.11 applies. $\square$

Corollary 6.35.2.22D3ABC6CFCD For a local analytic parametrization of a pure $p$-dimensional set $A$ by a proper finite projection $\pi :A\to \Delta '\subset \mathbb C^ p$ with $m$ covering sheets off its discriminant and fiber $\pi ^{-1}(0)=\{ a\} $,

\[ \nu ([A],\log |\pi |^2)=m. \]

Proof. The finite projection of finite local parametrization of analytic germs and the zero-singular-mass convention of theorem 5.61.2 give $\pi _*[A]=m[\Delta ']$: change variables on its covering sheets; the discriminant and its proper analytic inverse image have zero regular-locus volume. Apply the projection formula and the ordinary normalization $\nu ([\mathbb C^ p],0)=1$ from theorem 6.33.1. For $p=0$ this is the direct finite point count. This identifies the projection-weight number; identifying it with the ordinary ambient number requires the additional cone/projection comparison. $\square$
