Theorem 6.25.8B766118DB089

Theorem 6.25.8.B766118DB089 Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb C^ n$ be pseudoconvex, let $2\leq q\leq n$, and let $\varphi \in C^\infty (\Omega )\cap \operatorname {PSH}(\Omega )$. If a measurable $(0,q)$-form $g$ is distributionally $\bar\partial $-closed and

\[ M=\int _\Omega |g|_{B_ q(\varphi )^{-1}}^2 \mathrm e^{-\varphi }\, \mathrm dV<\infty , \]

there is a $(0,q-1)$-form $u$ with $\bar\partial u=g$ and $\| u\| _\varphi ^2\leq M$. No global $L^2(\mathrm e^{-\varphi })$ assumption on $g$ is required. The degree-one statement is Hörmander's smooth-weight $L^2$ estimate.

Proof. If $\Omega $ is empty the assertion is trivial. Otherwise the dual-energy lower bound and boundedness above of $\varphi $ on compact sets imply $g\in L^2_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Let $s\geq 0$ be the smooth strictly plurisubharmonic exhaustion supplied by Oka's boundary-distance criterion; this includes arbitrary unbounded or disconnected open sets and does not require boundary regularity.

We use the auxiliary functions constructed in the proof of Hörmander's smooth-weight $L^2$ estimate, the auxiliary convex weight inequalities. More precisely, for each integer $t\geq 1$ that construction gives smooth $\psi \geq 0$, cutoffs as in lemma 6.25.5, and a nonnegative convex nondecreasing $\gamma $, with

\[ \gamma (s)=\psi =0\quad \hbox{on }\{ s\leq t\} ,\qquad \gamma (s)\geq 2\psi ,\qquad H_{\gamma (s)}\geq (1+t)|\bar\partial \psi |^2\operatorname {Id}, \]

and

9B38BB042909 6.25.9
\begin{equation} \int _\Omega |g|^2\mathrm e^{-\varphi -\gamma (s)+\psi }\, \mathrm dV<\infty . \end{equation}

To verify the scope of this reuse, the cutoffs there are translates of a fixed one-variable cutoff composed with $s$, and $\psi $ vanishes with its derivative on $\{ s\leq t+1\} $. On each compact band $\{ t+l\leq s\leq t+l+1\} $ the construction uses only a positive lower bound for $H_ s$, bounds for $\psi ,\bar\partial \psi $, and the finite number $\int |g|^2\mathrm e^{-\varphi +\psi }$. A locally finite sum of nondecreasing smooth steps chooses $\gamma '$ above these bounds and makes its preceding integral at least the logarithm needed for a $2^{-l}$ integral bound. The number or degree of the coefficients of $g$ does not enter; their squared norm in this integral is all that is used. Thus the stated construction applies unchanged here.

Put $\Phi =\varphi +\gamma (s)$ and $\Phi _ k=\Phi +(k-3)\psi $. By (6.25.9), $g$ belongs to the middle Hilbert space and to $\ker S$. Since $B_ q$ is linear and (6.25.2) gives $B_ q(\gamma (s))\geq q(1+t)|\bar\partial \psi |^2\operatorname {Id}$, (6.25.7) implies

\[ \int \langle B_ q(\varphi )v,v\rangle \mathrm e^{-\Phi }\, \mathrm dV \leq (1+t^{-1})\| T^*v\| _{\Phi _1}^2+\| Sv\| _{\Phi _3}^2. \]

The dual-energy Cauchy–Schwarz inequality and $\gamma (s)\geq 2\psi $ now give

\[ \begin{split} |\langle g,v\rangle _{\Phi _2}|^2 & \leq M\int \langle B_ q(\varphi )v,v\rangle \mathrm e^{\varphi -2\Phi _2}\, \mathrm dV\\ & \leq M\int \langle B_ q(\varphi )v,v\rangle \mathrm e^{-\Phi }\, \mathrm dV\\ & \leq M(1+t^{-1}) \bigl (\| T^*v\| _{\Phi _1}^2+\| Sv\| _{\Phi _3}^2\bigr ). \end{split} \]

Initially $v$ is smooth and compactly supported. Both sides are continuous for the joint graph norm, so lemma 6.25.5 extends this inequality to the full domains. Hilbert-complex solvability from the dual energy estimate gives $\bar\partial u_ t=g$ with $\| u_ t\| _{\Phi _1}^2\leq M(1+t^{-1})$. On $\{ s<t\} $ the weight $\Phi _1$ is exactly $\varphi $.

For every integer $l\geq 1$ the $u_ t$, $t\geq l$, are bounded in $L^2(\{ s<l\} ,\mathrm e^{-\varphi })$. A diagonal weak subsequence by weak subsequences in Hilbert spaces gives compatible limits on all these sublevels, because restriction is a bounded linear map. Their union is $u$, and weak convergence there implies distributional convergence on $\Omega $, so $\bar\partial u=g$. Weak lower semicontinuity gives $\int _{\{ s<l\} }|u|^2\mathrm e^{-\varphi }\leq M$ for every $l$; monotone convergence proves the required global estimate. $\square$
