Proposition 9.21.49028D1B15EA2

Proposition 9.21.4.9028D1B15EA2 Let $D\subset \mathbb C^ n$ be a bounded domain of holomorphy. If $\mathcal O(D)\simeq \mathcal O(\Delta ^ n)$ as Fréchet spaces, then $D$ has a negative PSH exhaustion and is hyperconvex.

Proof. Let $T:\mathcal O(\Delta ^ n)\to \mathcal O(D)$ be the isomorphism and put $f_\alpha =T(z^\alpha )$. For $\alpha \ne 0$ set $v_\alpha =|\alpha |^{-1}\log |f_\alpha |$. On every compact subset $K\Subset D$, continuity of $T$ gives constants $C_ K>0$, $r_ K<1$ such that $\sup _ K|f_\alpha |\leq C_ Kr_ K^{|\alpha |}$. The regularized tail envelopes

\[ u_ m=\left(\sup _{|\alpha |\geq m}v_\alpha \right)^*,\qquad \rho =\lim _{m\to \infty }u_ m \]

are PSH and decrease; their limit is PSH or identically $-\infty $. The local uniform bound just obtained gives $\rho <0$ everywhere, and $\rho (z)\geq \limsup _{|\alpha |\to \infty }v_\alpha (z)$.

Fix $0<r<1$ and choose $r<r_1<r_2<1$. If $\rho (z)\leq \log r$, there is a finite constant $A_ z$ such that $|f_\alpha (z)|\leq A_ zr_1^{|\alpha |}$ for every multi-index, including $\alpha =0$ after enlarging $A_ z$. For $g\in \mathcal O(D)$, expand $T^{-1}g=\sum c_\alpha z^\alpha $. Cauchy's coefficient estimate on the polydisc of radius $r_2$ gives

\[ |g(z)|\leq A_ z\sum _\alpha |c_\alpha |r_1^{|\alpha |} \leq A_ zC_{r_1,r_2}\| T^{-1}g\| _{r_2\overline{\Delta }^ n}. \]

The Taylor expansion may be evaluated after $T$ because it converges in the compact-open topology and $T$ is continuous. Continuity of $T^{-1}$ gives one compact $L\Subset D$ and $C'>0$, depending on $r_2$ but not on $z$ or $g$, such that the last norm is at most $C'\| g\| _ L$.

Apply this bound to $g^ k$, where $k$ is an arbitrary positive integer. Taking $k$th roots and letting $k\to \infty $ eliminates the finite constant $A_ zC_{r_1,r_2}C'$ and proves $|g(z)|\leq \| g\| _ L$. Although $T$ need not preserve multiplication, it applies to every $g^ k$ with the same estimate. Hence

\[ \{ \rho \leq \log r\} \subset \widehat L_{\mathcal O(D)}\Subset D. \]

The last compactness is holomorphic convexity of a domain of holomorphy. If $\rho $ were identically $-\infty $, this inclusion would put the whole noncompact open domain $D$ in one compact subset of $D$, which is impossible. Thus $\rho $ is a negative PSH exhaustion. Apply proposition 7.25.1 to obtain a continuous negative PSH exhaustion, proving hyperconvexity. $\square$
