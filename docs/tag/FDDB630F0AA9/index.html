Lemma 7.31.1FDDB630F0AA9

Lemma 7.31.1.FDDB630F0AA9 Suppose a neighborhood of a compact subset $K$ of a complex manifold carries a smooth strictly plurisubharmonic function $\rho $. Every plurisubharmonic function defined near $K$ is, on a fixed smaller neighborhood of $K$, the decreasing limit of smooth plurisubharmonic functions.

Proof. We first localize the finite-cover proof of decreasing smooth regularization with a vanishing curvature error. For bounded $v$ on a slightly larger compact neighborhood and fixed $\eta >0$, that proof gives smooth $a_{\eta ,\delta }\downarrow v$ on a fixed open neighborhood $O$ of $K$, with $dd^ ca_{\eta ,\delta }\geq -\eta \, dd^ c\rho $. Here is why no compact-without-boundary assumption is needed for this use. Take finitely many nested charts $V_ i\Subset W_ i\Subset U_ i$ whose $V_ i$ cover a fixed larger compact neighborhood. The bounded coordinate-convolution comparison gives uniformly vanishing overlap errors. Add fixed small chart penalties, zero on $V_ i$ and negative near $\partial W_ i$, and take the smooth regularized maximum. Where a branch leaves $W_ i$ in $\overline O$, another branch has zero penalty and dominates it; the removal property of that maximum glues the formulas. All relevant boundaries intersecting $\overline O$ are compact. The curvature error is at most $\eta \, dd^ c\rho $ after choosing the penalties small enough. For the fixed cover and penalties the local convolutions and maximum decrease with $\delta $, and the zero-penalty branches give limit $v$ and the majorant inequality. These are exactly the local steps of the cited proof, on the fixed buffered compact neighborhood.

Add a constant to $\rho $ so that $\rho \geq 0$ on that neighborhood. Put $v_ N=\max \{ u,-N\} $, choose $t_ N\downarrow 0$, and use the preceding construction with $\eta _ N=t_ N/2$. Choose its smoothing parameter so that

\[ w_ N=a_{\eta _ N,\delta _ N}+t_ N\rho +2^{-N} \]

decreases strictly with $N$ on a fixed compact neighborhood of $K$, and $\| a_{\eta _ N,\delta _ N}-v_ N\| _{L^1}\leq 2^{-N}$ there. The choice is possible inductively: the pointwise limit of the candidate functions for index $N$ is $v_ N+t_ N\rho +2^{-N}$, below the previous smooth majorant by at least $2^{-N}$. Increasing open sets where the candidate lies below that previous function cover the compact set, so one sufficiently small parameter works everywhere. The $L^1$ condition is the bounded decreasing convergence for this fixed $v_ N$. Each $w_ N$ is smooth plurisubharmonic, since $dd^ cw_ N\geq (t_ N/2)dd^ c\rho $. Also $w_ N\geq u$, and $w_ N\to u$ in $L^1$, because $v_ N\downarrow u$ in $L^1$. The decreasing plurisubharmonic limit is therefore the canonical representative $u$ at every point, by decreasing nets of plurisubharmonic functions. Restriction to the interior of the fixed compact neighborhood proves the lemma. $\square$
