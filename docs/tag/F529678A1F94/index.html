Proposition 2.14.7F529678A1F94

Proposition 2.14.7.F529678A1F94 Suppose there is a commuting inverse sequence of short exact sequences of Fréchet spaces

\[ 0\longrightarrow A_\nu \xrightarrow {i_\nu }B_\nu \xrightarrow {p_\nu }C_\nu \longrightarrow 0, \]

where $i_\nu $ identifies $A_\nu $ with the closed kernel of $p_\nu $. If $A_{\nu +1}\to A_\nu $ has dense image for every $\nu $, then

\[ 0\longrightarrow \varprojlim A_\nu \longrightarrow \varprojlim B_\nu \longrightarrow \varprojlim C_\nu \longrightarrow 0 \]

is strictly exact: the first arrow is a closed topological embedding and the last is open and surjective. Density of the transitions on $B_\nu $ or $C_\nu $ is not required.

Proof. Choose coherent complete metrics on the $B_\nu $. Fix $c=(c_\nu )\in \varprojlim C_\nu $ and a lift $b_0$ of $c_0$. Given a lift $b_\nu $, take any lift $v\in B_{\nu +1}$ of $c_{\nu +1}$. Commutativity makes $r_\nu v-b_\nu $ an element of $i_\nu (A_\nu )$. Density of $A_{\nu +1}\to A_\nu $ and continuity of $i_\nu $ give $a\in A_{\nu +1}$ such that $b_{\nu +1}=v-i_{\nu +1}a$ satisfies

\[ d_\nu (r_\nu b_{\nu +1},b_\nu )<2^{-\nu -1}. \]

It remains a lift of $c_{\nu +1}$. Coordinate limits as in lemma 2.14.2 give a compatible $b\in \varprojlim B_\nu $, and $p_\mu b_\mu =c_\mu $ follows by continuity from every term of the $\mu $-coordinate sequence. This proves surjectivity. The map is open by proposition 2.12.1, since both limits are Fréchet. Its kernel is precisely the inverse limit of the closed kernels $i_\nu (A_\nu )$; the coordinate topological identifications identify this closed subspace with $\varprojlim A_\nu $, including its topology. $\square$
