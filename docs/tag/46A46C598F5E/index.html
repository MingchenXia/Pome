Lemma 2.16.146A46C598F5E

Lemma 2.16.1.46A46C598F5E Let $U$ be a relatively compact coordinate neighborhood, with all coefficients below smooth on a neighborhood of $\overline U$. Let $g$ be a Riemannian metric. Suppose a real smooth function $\phi $ satisfies on $\overline U$, for constants $a\geq 0$, $b>0$,

9F94A6DC571E 2.16.2
\begin{equation} \operatorname {Hess}_ g\phi \geq -ag,\qquad \operatorname {Hess}_ g\phi (W,W)-a|W|_ g^2\geq b, \quad W=\operatorname {grad}_ g\phi . \end{equation}

If on $U$

\[ P=-\Delta _ g I_ N+\sum _ jB_ j(x)\partial _ j+C(x) \]

with smooth complex matrix coefficients, there are $t_0,C_0>0$ such that, for $t\geq t_0$ and $w\in C_ c^\infty (U,\mathbb C^ N)$,

B6BBF68CEF95 2.16.3
\begin{equation} t^3\int _ Ue^{2t\phi }|w|^2\, \mathrm dV_ g +t\int _ Ue^{2t\phi }|dw|_ g^2\, \mathrm dV_ g \leq C_0\int _ Ue^{2t\phi }|Pw|^2\, \mathrm dV_ g. \end{equation}

The constants depend on the metric, weight and coefficient bounds on $\overline U$, and are independent of $w,t$.

Proof. First take $P_0=-\Delta _ g I_ N$. For $v=e^{t\phi }w$ the conjugate operator is $P_ tv=(S+A)v$, where

\[ S=-\Delta _ g-t^2|W|^2,\qquad A=2tW\cdot \operatorname {grad}_ g+t\Delta _ g\phi . \]

Integration by parts gives $S^*=S$ and $A^*=-A$. Thus

E33711D80A52 2.16.4
\begin{equation} \| P_ tv\| ^2=\| Sv\| ^2+\| Av\| ^2 +4t\int \operatorname {Hess}_ g\phi (\nabla v,\nabla \bar v) +4t^3\int \operatorname {Hess}_ g\phi (W,W)|v|^2 -t\int (\Delta _ g^2\phi )|v|^2. \end{equation}

All integrals here and below use $\mathrm dV_ g$, and the derivative terms are summed over the $N$ components. Here is the integration calculation. The cross term involving $-\Delta _ g$ is $2\operatorname {Re}\langle -\Delta _ gv,Av\rangle $. Differentiate $Av$ once after integration by parts. The derivative of $W$ gives $4t\operatorname {Hess}_ g\phi (\nabla v,\nabla \bar v)$. The remaining second derivatives of $v$ give $2tW(|\nabla v|^2)$, whose integral cancels $2t(\Delta _ g\phi )|\nabla v|^2$. The last term is $t\langle d|v|^2,d\Delta _ g\phi \rangle $, whose integral is $-t(\Delta _ g^2\phi )|v|^2$. For the multiplication term in $S$, the commutator with $A$ is $2t^3W(|W|^2)=4t^3\operatorname {Hess}_ g\phi (W,W)$. This proves (2.16.4). These identities also follow directly from $\Delta _ gf=|g|^{-1/2}\partial _ i(|g|^{1/2}g^{ij}\partial _ jf)$ and $\operatorname {Hess}_ g\phi {}_{ij} =\partial _ i\partial _ j\phi -\Gamma ^ k_{ij}\partial _ k\phi $, with $\Gamma ^ k_{ij}=g^{k\ell }(\partial _ ig_{j\ell } +\partial _ jg_{i\ell }-\partial _\ell g_{ij})/2$.

Put $K=\sup _{\overline U}|\Delta _ g^2\phi |$. Since

\[ \| \nabla v\| ^2=\operatorname {Re}\langle Sv,v\rangle +t^2\int |W|^2|v|^2, \]

the Hessian bounds in (2.16.4) give

\begin{align*} \| P_ tv\| ^2 & \geq \| Sv\| ^2+\| Av\| ^2 -4at\operatorname {Re}\langle Sv,v\rangle +(4bt^3-Kt)\| v\| ^2\\ & \geq \tfrac 12\| Sv\| ^2+\| Av\| ^2 +(4bt^3-8a^2t^2-Kt)\| v\| ^2. \end{align*}

For all sufficiently large $t$ this controls $\tfrac 12\| Sv\| ^2+2bt^3\| v\| ^2$. The same gradient identity, Cauchy–Schwarz and the boundedness of $W$ then give

\[ t\| \nabla v\| ^2 \leq \tfrac 12\| Sv\| ^2+\tfrac 12t^2\| v\| ^2 +t^3\sup |W|^2\| v\| ^2 \leq C\| P_ tv\| ^2. \]

Since $e^{t\phi }dw=dv-tv\, d\phi $, this proves (2.16.3) for $P_0$.

On the coordinate compact set the remaining coefficients obey $|(P-P_0)w|\leq M(|dw|_ g+|w|)$. Apply the estimate for $P_0$ and use $|P_0w|^2\leq 2|Pw|^2+2M^2(|dw|_ g+|w|)^2$. Increasing $t_0$ absorbs the two lower-order weighted integrals into the left side. This proves the stated matrix-coupled estimate. $\square$
