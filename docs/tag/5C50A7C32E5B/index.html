Theorem 7.26.35C50A7C32E5B

Theorem 7.26.3.5C50A7C32E5B Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb C^ n$ and $1\leq k\leq n$. For PSH functions $u_1,\ldots ,u_ k$ not identically $-\infty $ on any component, the generalized form

\[ dd^ c\iota u_1\wedge \cdots \wedge dd^ c\iota u_ k \]

is well defined coefficientwise in $\mathcal G(\Omega )$. If all $u_ j$ are locally bounded, it is associated with their classical Bedford–Taylor product. In particular the generalized Monge–Ampère form is associated with $(dd^ cu)^ n$ for locally bounded $u$.

Proof. PSH functions with the stated component convention are locally integrable distributions. Their embedded derivatives are moderate, and the finite polynomial defining a wedge product is moderate. Changing representatives changes it by a negligible form, because $\mathcal N$ is a differential ideal. This proves existence without assuming a distributional limit for unbounded inputs.

Suppose now that the inputs are locally bounded. We prove convergence for every fixed real kernel $\varphi \in \mathcal A_0$, a stronger assertion than association. Choose a nonzero nonnegative smooth compact kernel $h$ with $\varphi +h\geq 0$. Such an $h$ is obtained from a sufficiently large bump equal to a positive constant on $\operatorname {supp}\varphi $. Set

\[ a_- =\int h>0,\quad a_+=1+a_-,\qquad \rho _-=h/a_-,\quad \rho _+=(\varphi +h)/a_+. \]

These are nonnegative mass-one kernels, and

\[ u_ j*S_\varepsilon \varphi =a_+u_ j*S_\varepsilon \rho _+ -a_-u_ j*S_\varepsilon \rho _-. \]

Each positive convolution is locally uniformly bounded PSH and converges locally in capacity to $u_ j$, by the lemma. Expand the smooth mixed product into its $2^ k$ terms. For each term the existing Bedford–Taylor continuity theorem for bounded capacity convergence gives weak convergence to $dd^ cu_1\wedge \cdots \wedge dd^ cu_ k$. The sum of the coefficients is $(a_+-a_-)^ k=1$. Therefore

\[ dd^ c(u_1*S_\varepsilon \varphi )\wedge \cdots \wedge dd^ c(u_ k*S_\varepsilon \varphi ) \longrightarrow dd^ cu_1\wedge \cdots \wedge dd^ cu_ k. \]

Apply the sequential continuity theorem along any sequence $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$ to justify the full parameter limit. Weak convergence of these forms is precisely association of their coefficients, completing the proof. $\square$
