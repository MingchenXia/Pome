Lemma 3.10.556A174D59425

Lemma 3.10.5.56A174D59425 Let $\rho $ be a finite positive measure on a compact Riemannian manifold of real dimension $m$. Suppose $\rho (B_ r(x))\leq A r^{m-2}$ for all $x$ and $0<r\leq r_0$, where $m\geq 2$. Then

\[ 0\leq h_ t\rho (x)\leq C(A+\rho (X))/t,\qquad 0<t\leq 1. \]

If $F\in L^1(X,\mathrm dV)$ is nonnegative, then

\[ t\int _ X (h_ t\rho )F\, \mathrm dV\longrightarrow 0. \]

Proof. For $t<r_0^2$, split the Gaussian kernel bound into the ball $B_{\sqrt t}(x)$ and the annuli with radii $2^ j\sqrt t$, as long as they are below $r_0$. Their contributions are bounded respectively by $CA/t$ and $CA\, 2^{(j+1)(m-2)}e^{-4^ j/C}/t$. The rest is bounded by $Ct^{-m/2}e^{-r_0^2/(Ct)}\rho (X)$, which is at most $C\rho (X)/t$. The remaining times are bounded by the same assertion after enlarging $C$. For the last claim, truncate $F$ at a number $M$. Conservation of the scalar heat integral gives

\[ t\int (h_ t\rho )F \leq C(A+\rho (X))\int _{\{ F>M\} }F\, \mathrm dV +Mt\, \rho (X). \]

First let $t$ tend to zero, then let $M$ tend to infinity. $\square$
