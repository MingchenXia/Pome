Corollary 19.10.1F31884F26A29

Corollary 19.10.1.F31884F26A29 Let $L$ be a smooth Hermitian holomorphic line bundle on a compact Hermitian complex $n$-manifold. If $\mathrm i\Theta _ L\geq 0$, then

2B3916324C79 19.10.2
\begin{equation} h^0(X,L^ k)=\frac{k^ n}{n!}\int _ X\Omega _ L^ n+o(k^ n). \end{equation}

If its curvature is positive definite at one point, the coefficient is positive, so $h^0(X,L^ k)\geq c k^ n$ for one $c>0$ and all sufficiently large integers $k$. The base is not assumed Kähler.

Proof. Take $F$ trivial. Semipositivity makes $I_ q=0$ for $q\geq 1$ and $I_0=\int _ X\Omega _ L^ n/n!$. The weak degree-zero inequality gives the upper bound. The strong inequality for $q=1$ gives $\liminf k^{-n}h_ k^0\geq I_0$, since $h_ k^1\geq 0$. For $n=1$ the same inequality is the Euler equality, so it applies also in that endpoint. Thus the limit is $I_0$. Strict positivity at one point persists on a nonempty open set and its top form has positive density there; the integral is positive. Taking any $0<c<I_0$ proves the stated eventual lower bound. $\square$
