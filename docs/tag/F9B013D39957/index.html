Definition 20.7.2F9B013D39957

Definition 20.7.2.F9B013D39957 A meromorphic section of a holomorphic vector bundle is given in holomorphic frames by vectors of meromorphic functions, compatible with the frame changes. Here a meromorphic function is locally a quotient of holomorphic functions with nonzero denominator germ. Holomorphic invertible transition matrices preserve such vectors in both directions, so the condition is independent of holomorphic frame. Away from their poles the vectors give an ordinary holomorphic section. No invariance under arbitrary smooth frame changes is claimed.
