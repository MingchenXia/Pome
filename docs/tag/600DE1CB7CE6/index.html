Lemma 9.51.9600DE1CB7CE6

Lemma 9.51.9.600DE1CB7CE6 For arbitrary open $G,U$ in an analytic space, their ordinary sheaf Mayer–Vietoris sequence has continuous maps for these cohomology topologies. Whenever its cohomology difference map is surjective, that map is open, including on non-Hausdorff quotient cohomology.

Proof. Take a countable distinguished acyclic basis $\mathcal W$ on $G\cup U$, retaining members contained in $G$ or $U$. Let $\mathcal W_ G,\mathcal W_ U$ be the subfamilies contained in $G,U$, and $\mathcal W_0=\mathcal W_ G\cap \mathcal W_ U$. They are acyclic covers of $G,U,G\cap U$. Put $A=\check C^\bullet (\mathcal W_ G,\mathcal F)\oplus \check C^\bullet (\mathcal W_ U,\mathcal F)$, $B=\check C^\bullet (\mathcal W_0,\mathcal F)$, and let $r:A\to B$ be restriction difference. Form the complex

\[ K^ n=A^ n\oplus B^{n-1},\qquad D(a,b)=(da,ra-db),\qquad B^{-1}=0. \]

The map $\check C^\bullet (\mathcal W,\mathcal F)\to K$ given by the two restrictions and zero in $B^{n-1}$ is continuous and induces the ordinary Mayer–Vietoris identification $H^*(K)=H^*(G\cup U,\mathcal F)$. Here is an explicit verification of this identification. Resolve $\mathcal F$ by its checked Godement resolution $\mathcal I^\bullet $. On each of the three acyclic covers, the augmented resolution columns are exact, as in the acyclic-cover comparison. Thus the double total with entries $K^ n(\mathcal I^ j)$ computes $H^*(K(\mathcal F))$. For fixed $j$, flabby Cech acyclicity computes the row's cohomology as that of

\[ \mathcal I^ j(G)\oplus \mathcal I^ j(U) \longrightarrow \mathcal I^ j(G\cap U). \]

The last map is onto by flabbiness and its kernel is $\mathcal I^ j(G\cup U)$ by gluing. Hence these rows are exact after that augmentation. The actual finite-diagonal comparison lemma identifies the same total with $\mathcal I^\bullet (G\cup U)$. The continuous map above, used also on Godement terms, agrees with the identity on that augmented resolution. It therefore induces this canonical cohomology bijection. Both source and target are Fréchet complexes, so cohomological openness makes it a topological identification.

The degreewise strict sequence $0\to B[-1]\to K\to A\to 0$ gives the continuous cohomology connecting maps: pull back the last complex's closed cycle space, use the open surjection from that Fréchet preimage, and descend the lifted differential. This is the continuous long-exact construction already proved in section 9.41. The lift formula and its Godement comparison give the usual sheaf Mayer–Vietoris maps, up to the common choice of difference sign. The difference map is induced by the continuous cochain map $r:A\to B$; if it is onto on cohomology, theorem 2.12.5 makes it open. $\square$
