Theorem 6.28.2E15FB3F8A946

Theorem 6.28.2.E15FB3F8A946 Let $(X,\omega )$ be complete Kähler, let $(E,h)$ be a smooth Hermitian holomorphic bundle, and let $1\leq q\leq n$. Fix $0\leq p\leq n$. Suppose $B=[\mathrm i\Theta (E),\Lambda _\omega ]\geq 0$ in bidegree $(p,q)$. If $F$ is locally $L^2$, is closed distributionally, and has finite inverse energy $M=\int _ X|F|_{B^{-1}}^2\, \mathrm dV_\omega $, then

48191F980D4B 6.28.3
\begin{equation} D''u=F,\qquad \| u\| _2^2\leq M \end{equation}

for an $L^2$ form $u$ of type $(p,q-1)$. Global ordinary $L^2$ integrability of $F$ is not required.

If $X$ merely possesses a complete Kähler metric and $\omega $ is any Kähler metric, the same conclusion holds in degree $(n,q)$ provided $E$ is $m$-semipositive with $m\geq \min (n-q+1,\operatorname {rank}E)$. The norm and inverse energy in this conclusion use the original $\omega $. Smooth data admit a smooth choice in either assertion.

Proof. For compactly supported smooth $v$, curvature Cauchy–Schwarz and theorem 10.33.2 give

\[ |\langle F,v\rangle |^2 \leq M\int _ X\langle Bv,v\rangle \, \mathrm dV_\omega \leq M\bigl (\| \delta ''v\| _2^2+\| D''v\| _2^2\bigr ). \]

Define $Av=(\delta ''v,D''v)$ in the direct sum of degrees $(p,q-1)$ and $(p,q+1)$. The displayed bound makes pairing with $F$ a well-defined bounded functional on $\operatorname {Ran}A$. Hilbert representation on its closure gives $(u,w)$ in that closure, with $\| u\| _2^2+\| w\| _2^2\leq M$, and

\[ F=D''u+\delta ''w \]

distributionally. In top barred degree the second space is zero. Otherwise $w$ is an $L^2$ limit of forms $D''v_ j$, hence $D''w=0$ distributionally. Applying $D''$ to the last identity, using $D''F=0$, gives $D''\delta ''w=0$. Thus $w$ is distributionally harmonic and is smooth by lemma 6.28.1.

Take the complete gradient cutoffs of lemma 6.26.1. Integrating the harmonic equation against $\chi _ R^2w$ and using $D''w=0$ gives

\[ \| \chi _ R\delta ''w\| _2^2 \leq 2\| \chi _ R\delta ''w\| _2\, \| (\bar\partial \chi _ R\wedge )^*w\| _2. \]

All integrals here have compact support; no prior global derivative bound is assumed. It follows that $\| \chi _ R\delta ''w\| _2^2\leq C R^{-2}\| w\| _2^2$. Letting $R\to \infty $ on each compact set proves $\delta ''w=0$, and establishes (6.28.3).

For the original-metric assertion put $\omega _\varepsilon =\omega +\varepsilon \widehat\omega $. These metrics are complete. The full coefficient inequalities of lemma 6.27.1 show semipositivity and bound the inverse energy by $M$ for each $\omega _\varepsilon $. The first assertion applies to the locally $L^2$ datum for each of these metrics. The fixed-comparison-density weak limit in the proof of theorem 6.27.6 now applies verbatim: first take local weak limits; test the norm with fixed $\omega _{\varepsilon _ k}$ for indices $j\geq k$; then let $\varepsilon _ k\downarrow 0$ and exhaust $X$. It gives the original norm bound. This argument uses no global ordinary norm of $F$. Finally lemma 6.27.8 gives a smooth minimum-norm primitive for smooth data. $\square$
